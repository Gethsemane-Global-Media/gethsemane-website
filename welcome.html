What next?

Here is your journey from today to your first class. You have already done the first step.

  1. ✓ Done

    You registered

    Your details are with the GSOM admissions team, and you have an application number.

  2. 2 Do this now

    Check your email

    We have just sent you an email with your application number, your interview date and time, and your Google Meet link. If you can’t see it, look in your Spam or Promotions folder.

  3. 3 Interview

    Your interview on Google Meet

    A short, friendly conversation of about 15 minutes. We want to hear your story and why you want to join GSOM. Use the Meet link in your email at your booked time.

    • Join 5 minutes early
    • Find a quiet place
    • Camera on
    • Can’t make it? Reply to the email
  4. 4 Admission

    Your admission letter

    After a successful interview, you receive your official admission letter from the GSOM Coordinator, with everything you need to get started.

  5. 5 Onboarding

    Join the GSOM learning platform

    Your admission letter has your link to the GSOM learning platform. Completing it signs you in officially as a Wave 7 student.

  6. 6 February 2027

    Classes begin

    Eight months of growing in the Word, in prayer and in service, onsite at Fed Poly, Bida or online from wherever you are.

We’re glad you’re here.
