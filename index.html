Get to know us

Questions about Gethsemane

Who we are, how the church is organised, and how you can be part of it.

What does “Gethsemane” mean?

It comes from the Hebrew gat shemanim, “oil press.” Gethsemane is the garden where Jesus prayed on the night before the cross. We carry the name because we are a praying church, where the weight people bring is pressed into prayer.

What is GKNI?

Gethsemane Kingdom Network International. Gethsemane is an agenda of God, to bring re-order and restoration of the Kingdom of God to the earth and to men, and an experience: transformation into the likeness of Christ. Read more about us.

What are the arms of Gethsemane?

Gethsemane serves through four arms of expression: the School Ministry arm (including GSOM), the Mission and Welfare arm, the Church and Fellowship arm, and the Media arm. See all the arms.

How do I join an arm?

Come to a Sunday Service and speak with the welcome team, or send us a message with the form below and choose “Joining an arm.” Someone from that arm will reach out to you.

What is GSOM, and who can apply?

The Gethsemane School of Ministry is our training arm: an 8-month discipleship program with classes held online and in person. Courses include Pneumatology, Leadership, Ministry, Self Discovery, and Finance, and Wave 7 is now enrolling until 30 January 2027. Visit the GSOM page.

What are Oil Press and 7 Hours with the Lord?

Oil Press is our Thursday gathering from 5:00 to 6:30 PM, a time of prayer and the Word. On the last Saturday of each month, 7 Hours with the Lord is a day set apart to tarry in God’s presence.

Do I need to be a member to come?

No. Everyone is welcome at every gathering, just as you are.
